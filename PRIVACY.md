# Hubster AutoHub extension — privacy policy

*Last updated: 26 November 2026*

The Hubster AutoHub extension works only together with the Hubster desktop app on the same computer. It lets Hubster's AutoHub learn the tasks you repeat on the web, so it can suggest and run them for you with your approval.

## What it reads

While AutoHub's "Web pages in your browser" setting is on in Hubster, the extension reads, on the page in front:

- the page's address, title, headings and visible text (up to a limit) and labelled values such as "Order #: 1234";
- the buttons and links you click (their names);
- what you enter in form fields (up to a limit), only while Hubster's "What you type in apps" setting is on.

It never reads password fields, card numbers, security codes, hidden fields, any field of a form that has a password field, text you write in editors, private or incognito windows, or sites you exclude. Some sites are always excluded, such as password managers. Clicks or typing faked by a page's own scripts are ignored.

## Where it goes

Only to the Hubster app on the same computer, through the browser's native messaging. The extension sends nothing over the internet, to us or to anyone else. Hubster stores what it receives on your computer, encrypted with a key kept in your system's keyring, and uses it only for AutoHub.

## What it isn't used for

The use of information received by the Hubster AutoHub extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is used only to provide AutoHub's features. It is not sold or shared, not used for advertising or credit decisions, and not read by people.

## Your control

In Hubster → AutoHub → Privacy you can turn web recording off, exclude sites, see and export what was recorded, delete any part of it, or delete everything. The extension's own button can also exclude the site you're on. Removing the extension stops it at once.
